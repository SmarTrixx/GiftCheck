About GiftsChecker

We started with one conviction: nobody should lose money to a fraudulent gift card.

Built to make gift card fraud
a thing of the past.

GiftsChecker was founded in 2022 after our team witnessed first-hand how easily counterfeit and depleted gift cards slipped through peer-to-peer transactions. We built a verification engine that anyone — from a solo buyer to a large retailer — can use for free.

Today we support 21 of the world's most popular card brands, process over 1.2 million verifications a month, and maintain 99.9% uptime across a globally distributed infrastructure.
